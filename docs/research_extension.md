# Research Extension

## Research Theme

From Geometric Similarity to Geographical Understanding

## Research Motivation

The original graph-based clustering approach relies
on fixed geometric similarity rules.

However, geometrical similarity does not necessarily
represent geographical meaning or human spatial intent.

## Planned Research Questions

1. How sensitive is spatial clustering to similarity
   thresholds and geographical context?

2. How do position, scale and boundary uncertainty
   affect geographical object matching?

3. Can semantic information improve geometry-based
   spatial grouping?

4. How can geospatial workflows be independently
   validated?

## Planned Experiments

- Parameter sensitivity analysis
- Synthetic geometry experiments
- Comparison with baseline clustering methods
- Semantic-spatial similarity modelling
- Geospatial workflow validation

## Long-term Research Direction

Geographically grounded spatial reasoning and
reliable autonomous GIS.

## Status

Planned research. Proposed methods and experiments
are not yet completed.
